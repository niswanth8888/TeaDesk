# 🍃 Tea Desk

> **A Digital Tea Factory Management System for Modern Tea Estates**

Tea Desk is a comprehensive software solution designed to digitize and streamline the daily operations of tea factories. It centralizes leaf procurement, weighing, supplier management, production tracking, inventory, payroll, sales, and reporting into a single platform.

The goal of Tea Desk is to eliminate manual record keeping, reduce errors, improve transparency, and provide real-time insights for tea factory management.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🌿 Leaf Procurement Management

* Record green leaf weighment
* Automatic weight calculations
* Supplier-wise transaction history
* Daily, weekly, monthly reports
* Duplicate entry prevention

### 👤 Supplier Management

* Complete supplier profiles
* Contact information
* Estate/location details
* Historical leaf supply records
* Outstanding balance tracking

### 🏭 Factory Operations

* Daily leaf intake monitoring
* Production workflow management
* Tea processing records
* Batch tracking
* Factory performance analytics

### 📦 Inventory Management
